Establishing the L0 Baseline (Where the Game Starts)

This is where the architectural game begins. We do not start by asking clients about Salesforce objects; we ask them how they make money. By diagnosing their pain points across these fundamental L0 Sales Models (One-Time, Subscription, Usage, Milestone), we establish the foundation to later carve out the specific L1 Commercial Threads that will become the structural backbone of the entire RLM implementation.

Tracker L0 Sales Model Lifecycle Domain The Executive Pain (The Noise) Business Impact / KPI at Risk
o1 One-Time Fulfill "Physical hardware delivery is delayed, but we are already billing the customer, causing massive disputes." Poor Customer Experience (CX) / High Dispute Rate
s1 Subscription Quote / Renew "Reps are overriding standard discounts on renewals to save deals, and Finance is finding out too late." Gross Margin Erosion / Delayed Contract Cycle Time
s2 Subscription Quote "Upgrades mid-year require us to cancel the old contract and write a brand new one manually." Net Revenue Retention (NRR) / High Admin Overhead
u1 Usage-Based Bill "We are under-billing for consumption overages because the mediation data never syncs with the invoice on time." 10% Revenue Leakage / ASC 606 Audit Risk
