RCA Design and Execution

RCA Design and Execution contains three distinct roles, sometime overlapping, and always coordinating.

  • Solutions Architect (business + architecture)
  • Technical Architect (platform + engineering)
  • Hands-on Execution (delivery + implementation)

Revenue Cloud Advanced Solutions Architect

  • Revenue domain knowledge: Lead-to-Cash, CPQ, subscriptions, billing, renewals, amendments, and revenue operations.
  • Salesforce Revenue Cloud Advanced: Product Catalog, Pricing Procedures, Configuration, Contracts, Orders, Assets, and Revenue Cloud data model.
  • Enterprise architecture: Integration patterns, APIs, event-driven architecture, identity, security, and scalability.
  • Business consulting: Running discovery workshops, challenging assumptions, and translating business goals into scalable capabilities.
  • Delivery leadership: Guiding implementation teams, making architectural decisions, managing technical risk, and mentoring developers and consultants.

Salesforce Platform Technical Architect

  • Platform Architecture: Designing scalable Revenue Cloud architecture using Product Catalog, Pricing Engine, Configuration, Contracts, Orders, Assets, and lifecycle management.
  • Salesforce Platform Engineering: Designing Apex, Flow, OmniStudio, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Platform Events, Invocable Actions, and asynchronous processing for enterprise-grade solutions.
  • Integration Architecture: Designing API-led integrations using MuleSoft, REST, event-driven messaging, ERP, PLM, tax engines, billing platforms, and external pricing services.
  • Data & Performance Architecture: Designing scalable data models, security architecture, sharing, large data volumes, governor-limit optimization, and high-performance quote processing.
  • Technical Governance: Establishing development standards, CI/CD pipelines, DevOps practices, code reviews, release management, environment strategy, and technical quality assurance[cite.

Revenue Cloud Advanced Hands-on Execution

RCA Specific

  • Product Catalog Configuration: Building products, bundles, attributes, selling models, configuration rules, qualification rules, and reusable product structures.
  • Pricing & Commercial Rules: Implementing Pricing Procedures, price adjustments, discounting, approvals, promotions, subscription pricing, usage pricing, and commercial policies.
  • Revenue Process Implementation: Configuring Quotes, Contracts, Orders, Amendments, Renewals, Assets, and lifecycle automation across the Lead-to-Revenue process.

Generic Platform

  • Automation & Integrations: Developing Flows, Apex, OmniStudio components, APIs, MuleSoft integrations, platform events, and external system connectivity.
  • Testing & Deployment: Executing solution testing, integration testing, deployment planning, production support, troubleshooting, performance tuning, and release execution.


Additional Info for Revenue Cloud Advanced Solutions Architect

For a Salesforce Revenue Cloud Advanced (RCA) Solutions Architect, the role is much broader than configuring products or pricing. The architect is responsible for ensuring the entire Lead-to-Revenue process aligns with business goals and can scale across products, channels, and geographies.

Here are the top 5 responsibilities, in the order they typically deliver the most value:

1. Design the End-to-End Revenue Lifecycle Architecture ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

This is the architect's primary responsibility. Rather than designing Salesforce alone, you design how the entire revenue ecosystem works. Typical activities include understanding sales motions, designing the Quote-to-Cash process, defining product/pricing architecture, and planning renewals and integrations.

Deliverables:

  • Solution Architecture Diagram
  • Business Capability Map
  • End-to-End Revenue Flow
  • Target Architecture

2. Model Products, Pricing, and Commercial Rules ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Revenue Cloud Advanced is fundamentally a product and pricing platform. The architect decides product hierarchy, bundles, configuration rules, pricing procedures, and subscription models. This work determines whether future pricing changes require code or can be handled by business users.

3. Lead Integration Architecture ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Revenue Cloud rarely operates alone. The architect defines integrations with ERP, tax engines, billing systems, PLM, customer portals, and data warehouses. They determine the System of Record, API strategy, synchronization patterns, and data ownership[cite: 1].

4. Translate Business Requirements into Scalable Design ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

This is where architecture differs from business analysis. Instead of documenting requirements, the architect designs reusable capabilities, balancing flexibility, maintainability, performance, simplicity, and future scalability.

5. Lead Delivery, Governance, and Technical Direction ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

The architect provides technical leadership throughout the implementation. Responsibilities include solution reviews, technical decision-making, sprint guidance, risk identification, and ensuring adherence to architecture standards.
