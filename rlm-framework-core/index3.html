Phase 1

Maturity (Revenue Project Initiation & Scoping)

Before provisioning a Revenue Cloud sandbox, we anchor the engagement by establishing clear boundaries around quoting, pricing, and billing. This sets the primary monetization strategy and eliminates CPQ/Billing scope creep.

1. Strategic Revenue Leadership Realignment

Aligning with CROs and CFOs to lock primary monetization strategies, ARR targets, target sales models, and explicit Revenue Lifecycle Management guardrails.

Resources: Tool Vision Rubric, Painpoints Worksheet

Key Architectural Deliverable: Strategic Revenue Rubric (L1)

2. Product & Catalog Structural Scoping

Compiling pricing models, bundling rules, and product taxonomy to set boundaries for CPQ and Billing deep-dives before discovery workshops begin.

Resources: Tool Taxonomy, As-Is Customer Profile

Key Architectural Deliverable: Unified RLM Catalog & Pricing Taxonomy (L2/L3)
🤝

Core Milestone: Quote-to-Cash Alignment Workshop

A targeted session with product owners to review the baseline product catalog, determine what quoting variants are strictly in-scope versus out-of-scope, and map out requirements traceability rules.

Phase 2

Discovery (Base Reality & RLM Bridge)

Transitioning from high-level monetization goals to diagnosing pain points in quoting accuracy, discount approvals, and billing leakage. We map exact Quote-to-Cash operational pain to native Salesforce capabilities.

1. Sales & Finance SME Diagnostics

Engaging Sales Ops and Deal Desk SMEs to capture real-world CPQ execution, contract generation, and revenue recognition workflows based on Phase 1 taxonomy.

Resources: SME Workshops, A Day in Life Discovery

Key Architectural Deliverable: RLM Business Requirements Documentation (V1)

2. Pricing & Variant Modeling

Mapping complex discount schedules, usage-based pricing variants, amendments, and renewals against target Revenue Cloud capabilities to ensure system coverage.

Resources: RLM Business Processes Step Through, Process Variants Details

Key Architectural Deliverable: RCA Traceability Matrix (Gap Summary L4)
📊

Core Milestone: Revenue Operations Executive Readout

Formal presentation to leadership synthesizing current state CPQ/Billing deficits, ARM requirement coverage, and the validated transition plan into structural design.

Phase 3

Story (RLM Solution Design)

Pivoting from process gaps to designing the Salesforce RLM architecture. We define the core objects, map integrations with ERP and Tax engines, and structure the exact Order-to-Cash automation logic.

1. RLM Architecture & Object Engineering

Defining specific data models (Products, Quotes, Orders, Assets) and structuring integration dependencies for seamless ERP handoffs and Avalara/Vertex tax integrations.

Resources: RLM Solution Process Flows, RCA Object Variables

Key Architectural Deliverable: Formal RCA/ARM Solution Design

2. Tactical Build Distribution

Packaging CPQ pricing rules, OmniStudio processes, and Billing schedules into actionable User Stories for development and implementation teams.

Resources: Development Handoff, If RLM To Be In an If-Else-Loop

Key Architectural Deliverable: Q2C User Stories (L5) & Success Criteria (L6)
📐

Core Milestone: Revenue Architecture Baseline Lock

Final sign-off on Q2C integration architecture, product bundle structures, and User Story readiness, signaling the green light for Revenue Cloud configuration.

Phase 4

Transformation (Configuration & Build)

Mobilizing the Revenue Cloud blueprint into physical configuration. We build OmniStudio flows, test pricing engines, validate billing automation, and enforce strict governance for deployment.

1. Salesforce RLM & Data Platforms

Activating the technological stack required for ARM transformation, including core CPQ logic, advanced quoting, and unified data views.

Learn More: OmniStudio in RLM, Agentic AI for Sales, Data Cloud for Revenue

Environment Strategy: RCA Sandbox | Dev | Production

2. Order-to-Cash Execution Validation

Executing targeted use cases for complex quoting, contract amendments, renewals, and accurate invoice generation before final production transition.

Case Studies & Practice:

  • Tesla Model Y (Complex Bundles)
  • Thrive Events (Subscriptions)
  • IBM (Enterprise Contracts)

Core Milestone: Go-Live & Revenue Governance Transition

Successful push to production environments, activating the unified revenue engine, and enforcing strict governance to realize target KPIs and prevent revenue leakage.
