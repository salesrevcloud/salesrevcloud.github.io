Guided RLM Implementation Framework

The complete Master Playbook for Enterprise Revenue Lifecycle Management. Move from executive vision to agile execution without falling into the "Theoretical Agile" trap.

Phase 1

Pre-Discovery (The Aerial Recce)

Before provisioning a sandbox or interviewing a single sales rep, we establish clear boundaries. We define the executive priorities, diagnose the sales models, and lock the L1-L3 process scope to eliminate CPQ/Billing scope creep before it starts.

1. Executive Vision & Alignment

Aligning with CROs and CFOs to lock primary monetization strategies, ARR targets, and explicit Revenue Lifecycle Management guardrails.

  • RLM Executive Diagnostic Guide (L0)
  • Strategic Vision-to-Commercial Threads (L1)
Key Architectural Deliverable: The Diagnostic Matrix (L0), The Commercial Threads and Priority Matrix (L1)

2. Process Breakdown & Boundary Lock

Collaborating with department heads to map high-level Quote-to-Cash capabilities (Levels 1-3) and lock in the functional scope before deep-dive discovery begins.

  • L1->L2->L3 Process Breakdown
  • Alignment Workshop Guide
Key Architectural Deliverable: Process Breakdown Table (L2 and L3)

Core Milestone: Alignment Lock

A targeted session with process owners using a "Straw Man" catalog to explicitly define what Quote-to-Cash capabilities are strictly in-scope versus out-of-scope for the upcoming Discovery phase.

Phase 2

Discovery (Boots on the Ground)

Transitioning from high-level monetization goals to diagnosing the literal step-by-step pain points in quoting accuracy, discount approvals, and billing leakage.

1. SME Deep-Dive Diagnostics

Engaging Sales Ops and Deal Desk SMEs to capture real-world CPQ execution, contract generation, and revenue recognition workflows based strictly on the Phase 1 taxonomy.

  • SME Workshop Playbook
  • A Day in the Life (As-Is Capture)
Key Architectural Deliverable: Validated Process Pain Points

2. The 3D Gap Analysis

Deconstructing the identified pain points into three distinct dimensions (Process, System, and Data) to ensure the eventual architecture solves the human problem, not just the technical one.

  • 3D Gap Analysis Framework
  • Process Variant Details
Key Architectural Deliverable: The Baseline Delta (As-Is to To-Be)

Core Milestone: Revenue Operations Executive Readout

Formal presentation to leadership synthesizing current state CPQ/Billing deficits and securing validation to move into the structural design phase.

The Strategic Pivot

Escaping Theoretical Agile

We have mapped the boundaries and diagnosed the pain. Before we write a single Jira ticket, we must define how we build. We shift away from building isolated generic "features" and instead execute End-to-End Revenue Threads based strictly on Sales Models.

Read the Commercial-First RLM Approach
Phase 3

Solution Design & Story Mapping

Pivoting from process gaps to designing the Salesforce RLM architecture. This is where we define the core objects, OmniStudio logic, and translate the business intent into highly structured development tickets.

1. Requirements Traceability & Architecture

Deploying the master blueprint that proves every single line of code maps directly back to a Phase 1 C-level business goal. We define data models, system integration points, and the conceptual solution.

  • The 12-Phase Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)
  • RLM Solution Process Flows
Key Architectural Deliverable: Formal RLM Solution Design

2. Jira Translation & Sprint 0 Handoff

Packaging CPQ pricing rules, OmniStudio processes, and Billing schedules into actionable User Stories. Conducting the "Three Amigos" handoff with the Tech Lead to sequence the agile sprints.

  • Architecting the Perfect Jira Ticket
  • Sprint 0: The Tech Lead Handoff
Key Architectural Deliverable: Sequenced Jira Backlog (Given/When/Then)

Core Milestone: Revenue Architecture Baseline Lock

Final sign-off on the Q2C integration architecture and User Story readiness, signaling the green light for Revenue Cloud development.

Phase 4

Transformation (Agile Execution)

Mobilizing the blueprint into physical configuration following our 4-Stage Agile sequence. We build the data foundation, test the pricing engines, validate the guardrails, and enforce strict governance for deployment.

1. The RLM Build Sprints

Executing the build in strict sequence: Data Model -> OmniStudio Engine -> Advanced Approval Guardrails. This ensures the architecture is tested horizontally across the Quote-to-Invoice lifecycle.

  • OmniStudio & BRE in RLM
  • Data Cloud for Revenue
  • Agentic AI for Sales
Environment Strategy: RLM Sandbox → Dev → UAT → Production

2. UAT, Validation & Go-Live

Executing targeted use cases for complex quoting, contract amendments, and accurate invoice generation. Handing over governance playbooks to the internal Center of Excellence.

  • Practice: Tesla Model Y (Complex Bundles)
  • Practice: Thrive Events (Subscriptions)
  • Practice: IBM (Enterprise Contracts)
Key Architectural Deliverable: UAT Validation Scripts & CoE Handover

Core Milestone: Go-Live & Revenue Governance Transition

Successful push to production environments, activating the unified revenue engine, and enforcing strict governance to realize target KPIs.
